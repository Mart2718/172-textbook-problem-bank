## Problem 10  
`FN2-B-01` · Looking Ahead to Calculus · item type FN2.d

*Calculus idea:* Difference quotient = secant slope; as h → 0 it approaches the tangent slope (the derivative)

a) $h + 8$

b) $h = 0.1$: $8.1$, $h = 0.01$: $8.01$, $h = 0.001$: $8.001$. The values approach $8$.

c) $(3, 15)$ and $(3+h, h^{2} + 8 h + 15)$, that is, $(3, g(3))$ and $(3+h, g(3+h))$. As $h$ shrinks, the second point slides toward the first, and the secant slopes approach the slope of the tangent line at $3$, which is $8$.
